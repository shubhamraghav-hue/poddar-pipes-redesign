# Site teardowns

Concrete design/interaction notes per reference site. Captured as inspiration for water-infrastructure UI — study the *approach*, don't copy brand assets.

---

## Astral Pipes — `astralpipes.com`

Mature B2B/industrial brand. Prioritizes clarity, trust, and taxonomy over flashy motion.

- **Hero:** Full-width auto-rotating carousel of application photography (CPVC Pro, Drain Pro, tanks). Brand-narrative headline ("India's No.1 Pipe Brand") sits below the carousel; secondary CTAs are credential links ("25 Years Warranty", "About Astral Group") rather than one dominant conversion button.
- **Motion:** Restrained — gentle cross-fade carousel (~4–5s), subtle fade/slide-up section reveals. No heavy parallax. Stats shown statically (opportunity: animate them).
- **Products:** Rectangular linked cards (image + bold name + tagline); whole card is the click target. Responsive 3–4 col grid. Categories via a **12-category mega menu**, each with 3–6 subcategories. Navigation is hierarchical (category → subcategory → product); no faceted filtering.
- **Color:** Industrial blue/teal (~`#0066CC`), white backgrounds, charcoal text (~`#333`), light-gray section fills (~`#F5F5F5`). Low saturation = trustworthy.
- **Type:** Neutral geometric sans (Inter/Roboto-like). Bold 700–800 headlines 32–48px; body 400 at 14–16px, 1.5–1.6 line-height; nav/labels medium 500–600. Generous headline letter-spacing for premium feel.
- **Layout:** 12-col grid, 1200–1400px container, 60–100px section rhythm, uniform card padding.
- **Signature:** Expandable mega menu (50+ SKUs without clutter); **scale-stats box** ("31+ Countries, 8.5K+ Employees, 15 Manufacturing Units"); always-visible customer-care phone CTA; **multi-brand ecosystem** row (sister brands as linked cards).

## Westlake Pipe & Fittings — `westlakepipe.com`

Corporate, trustworthy, and the most inventive information architecture of the three.

- **Hero:** Full-width job-site photograph (pipe being carried) + authority headline ("Trusted Leader in PVC Pipe & Fittings"). Two clear CTAs above the fold: "Contact Us" and "Explore Our Products".
- **Motion:** Straightforward/professional; a GIF in the Parts Catalog section; clarity over effects.
- **Products / navigation:** **Six market segments shown as clickable hotspots on a pipe-system illustration** (Municipal, Plumbing & Industrial, Water Well, Agriculture & Irrigation, Pool & Spa, Electrical) — each links to a dedicated page. A **segment-selector modal on entry** lets users pre-filter their path. Resource cards (Price Lists, Certification Letters, Technical Specs) in simple rectangular layouts.
- **Color:** Professional blue + white; blue-green-white accents. No vibrant accents.
- **Type:** Modern sans; heavy heading weights/scale; legible body with generous line spacing. Corporate tone.
- **Layout:** 12-col grid, consistent headline → description → CTA section rhythm, generous vertical whitespace.
- **Signature:** **Interactive hotspot diagram** on a pipe illustration (schematic-as-navigation); **market-segment selector modal**; deep **Resources hub** with Quick Links (price lists, cert letters, drawings, installation guides, tech specs, bulletins, loading charts) and **five calculators**.

## Plumbing Solutions — `plumbingsolutionsonline.com`

Local service brand. Trust + conversion craft; more consumer-facing than the two manufacturers.

- **Hero:** Text-focused (little hero imagery). Direct headline ("Your Trusted, Local Plumber in Central NJ") with an **orange phone-CTA button** and a secondary "Schedule Online" — both above the fold.
- **Motion:** Staggered section reveals; **testimonial carousel** with prev/next; Instagram feed carousel with load-more; numeric social proof ("4.9 over 544 reviews").
- **Products/services:** 3-col image+title service cards (Water Heaters, Sump Pumps, Drain Cleaning…). **Success stories** as modular Solution / End Result / Review cards. **Coupon cards** with big dollar amounts, benefit lists, expiry, printable PDFs.
- **Color:** Teal/turquoise (~`#0D8B8B`) + burnt-orange CTAs (~`#E67E22`); white base, charcoal text; teal SVG icons and starburst badges.
- **Type:** Contemporary, approachable sans; bold section titles, readable ~16px body; all-caps used sparingly for small labels/coupon headers.
- **Layout:** 12-col grid, 40–60px section padding, center-aligned headers + left-aligned body, ~1000–1200px containers.
- **Signature:** **"Two-hour arrival window"** differentiator surfaced early; cohesive minimalist **SVG icon set** (wrench badges, smiley, scheduling); testimonial carousel; **coupon card** design; **25th-anniversary starburst** badge.

## Cross-site synthesis

| Dimension | Convergent pattern |
|---|---|
| Palette | Industrial blue/teal base + white space; optional single warm (orange) accent |
| Type | Geometric sans, bold headlines, high legibility, no serifs |
| Trust | Certifications, warranty, review counts, years/scale stats given prominence |
| Nav | Product taxonomy (mega menu) **and** segment/application entry points |
| Imagery | Real application/job-site photography over abstract graphics |
| Motion | Restrained by default; carousels + gentle reveals; one memorable interactive device |
| Memorable device | Manufacturers lean on schematic/diagram navigation + scale stats; service brand leans on social proof + offers |
